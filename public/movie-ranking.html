Best Movies According to Joel

My Top 3 Movies of All-Time

1. Interstellar

A movie that perfectly blends the magic of cinematography, story, themes, music, and set pieces. Interstellar is a science fiction epic that combines elements of science, religion, and love to evoke an incredibly powerful emotional story at the highest hollywood blockbuster level.

2. Prisoners

Prisoners is a film that is once in a lifetime. It is a mystery movie where the twists are almost impossible to see coming, even for the most seasoned movie-goers. A story of loss, grief, faith, and the toll our sins take on our lives, this film is tense and emotional.

3. La La Land

Strangely enough, a musical has somehow made its way on to my top 3. Unlike most musicals, the characters are actually justified in their preference for breaking out in song. This is a romantic comedy centered around the dreams of making it big, but at what cost? This movie forces viewers to look inward and ask, "Is the dream that I have just a fantasy? Am I already living the dream now? Will I dream of where I am now when I make to where I think I will want to be?" It hurts to imagine that our dreams are just fantasy, but the beauty of this movie is showing us all the dream is right in front of us. If only we would appreciate it.
