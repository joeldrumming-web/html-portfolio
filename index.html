Connecting Patient Outreach, Enrollment, and Retention.

Patient CRM · Healthcare SaaS · Engagement → Enrollment → Retention

The problem

Three teams. Disconnected tools. Repeated work.

Outreach, enrollment, and retention teams relied on separate tools and processes to manage different stages of the patient journey. That led to repeated data entry, difficulty seeing previous conversations, and extra coordination whenever work moved from one department to another.

My approach

Start with the people doing the work.

I interviewed department leaders and the employees handling daily outreach and enrollment activities to understand their different responsibilities and common problems. I used those findings to help shape three connected experiences, each tailored to its users while supporting easier handoffs and more consistent information across the patient lifecycle.

The connected workflow

→

Enrollment

Support enrollment specialists with a more continuous process, reducing the need to recreate information already gathered earlier.

→

Retention

Help teams manage ongoing patient relationships, changing needs, and timely follow-up with more information about previous interactions.

Connected Patient Data

More consistent information across handoffs

Helped design a connected patient journey in which important information could be shared between teams. We focused on reducing duplicate work, improving data quality, and making transitions more dependable without requiring each department to operate in the same way.

Cross-Team Communication

Better visibility into patient outreach

Recognized that scattered communication records made coordinated outreach difficult. Helped improve the ability for teams to see into prior patient interactions and follow-up needs, allowing staff to make better-informed decisions about when and how to reconnect.

Workflow Efficiency

Turning reporting into action

Worked on ways to make information from reports more useful in day-to-day patient management. The focus was on reducing repetitive administrative steps and helping teams respond more efficiently to groups of patients who needed attention.

Data Integrity & Accountability

Greater confidence in shared records

Identified the importance of clear ownership of work when multiple departments contribute to a shared patient record. Helped shape safeguards and visibility that supported reliable information, appropriate access, and confidence in how work was attributed.

Executive Decision Support

Helped define tailored views for different users, from frontline staff managing daily tasks to leaders monitoring team activity. The goal was to make priorities easier to act on and give decision-makers a clearer understanding of performance without overwhelming users with irrelevant information.

Designed business value

The project helped transform three disconnected departmental processes into a more connected experience for teams working with patients. Its design emphasized better handoffs between teams, less repeated administrative work, more informed outreach, stronger accountability, and more useful clearer reporting. These are qualitative contributions and intended benefits; no unverified numerical results are claimed.
